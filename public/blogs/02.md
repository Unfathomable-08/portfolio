## Why one person says 10k and another says 50k

They are not arguing about skill. They are selling different products. That is why the prices look so far apart, and why it feels confusing when you ask three people.

**PKR 10,000–20,000** is WordPress + a paid theme + your logo. It is fast and it is cheap. But you start paying for hosting (PKR 16,000–35,000 a year) the day it goes live, and that bill does not stop.

**PKR 25,000–40,000** is custom: your pages, your admin, WhatsApp, forms, SEO setup. On [most sites I build](https://dev-muhammad.vercel.app/web-development), hosting is free for life. It takes three to six weeks, because the work is made for your business and not copied from a template. This is the one I tell people to buy.

**PKR 50,000–80,000** is a custom store: products, COD, JazzCash, Easypaisa, bank transfer, order list. It is built for how your customers pay here. It is not a Shopify trial.

**PKR 100,000+** is a complete platform or software: POS, CRM, multi-branch. This is a different kind of work, and it is not a website package.

Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad the stack changes the price, not the city. A developer in one city does not charge more just because of the city. What changes the price is what you are building. WordPress and Shopify keep billing you. Custom does not, if hosting is included.

## WordPress: cheap setup, then PKR 16,000–35,000 hosting forever

The WordPress website cost in Pakistan is not the setup fee. The setup fee is just the door. What you pay after you walk in is the real cost.

You buy a theme. Then Elementor. Then security. Then backup. Then WooCommerce add-ons if you sell. Each one looks small when you buy it, but they all add up. And when an update breaks the layout, someone has to fix it, and that someone sends you a bill.

Hosting for a real WordPress site is **PKR 16,000–35,000 per year**. It is not the PKR 3,000 shared plan they mention in the first chat. Cheap hosting is why those sites die, because the site becomes slow and then it stops working. A lot of “cheap” sites also sit on the developer’s hosting, so you pay them every year or the site disappears.

Let us look at the two-year WordPress bill. The setup is PKR 10,000 – 20,000. Hosting is **PKR 16,000 – 35,000 per year**, which is PKR 32,000 – 70,000 over two years. Paid plugins take another PKR 10,000 – 30,000. And fixes take PKR 10,000 – 30,000.

So you can spend more than a custom site and still be on WordPress. That is the trap. You thought you were saving money, but you were only paying later, in small pieces. WordPress is fine for a blog. It is a bad place to put a business.

## Shopify: dollars every month, and the bill does not stop

Shopify does not charge in rupees. You pay **USD**. This is the first thing many Pakistani shop owners forget, and it matters a lot when the dollar goes up.

The basic Shopify plan is **$19/month**. In Pakistani rupees, that comes to about **PKR 65,000 a year** for the plan alone before apps and transaction fees.

**The plan alone is ~PKR 65,000 a year ($19/mo).** And that is before anything else, because there are more costs on top. You need apps for JazzCash / Easypaisa, because they are not built in. You need a theme. You pay 2% extra when you use a local gateway, because Shopify Payments is not in Pakistan. And you need more apps for COD.

The first year easily crosses PKR 100,000+ once you add apps. In year two you pay the dollar bill again. And you do not own the shop. If they freeze the account, the shop is gone.

Shopify is rent. Custom is a purchase. For a Pakistani store, custom wins unless you have a reason to live inside Shopify. Most shops here do not.

This is the option I build, and the one I tell you to take. I tell you this because it is the one that costs the least after the first year, not because it is the one I sell.

You own the code. WhatsApp, JazzCash, Easypaisa, COD all built for how people here actually pay. There is no plugin tax, and there is no USD bill at the end of the month.

On most [custom websites I ship](https://dev-muhammad.vercel.app/web-development), **hosting is included for life**. You still buy the domain (about PKR 3,000–5,000 a year for .com). That is it. Nothing else comes every year.

Now put the three side by side. WordPress hosting is PKR 16,000–35,000 every year. Shopify is ~PKR 65,000 every year in dollars for the plan alone. Custom, most of the time, is **PKR 0 hosting.**

Here is the two-year picture:

| Stack | Year 1 | Year 2 | You own it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| WordPress | Setup + **16k–35k hosting** + plugins | **16k–35k hosting** + plugins + fixes | Kind of |
| Shopify | USD plan ($19/mo ≈ 65k/yr) + apps | Same USD bill again | No |
| Custom + lifetime hosting | Build + domain | Domain | Yes |

Day-one custom looks expensive in WhatsApp. That is true, and I will not pretend it is not. But year-two custom is the cheapest of the three. That is why I do not start clients on WordPress or Shopify unless they insist, and even then I tell them they will pay more later.

## What cheap quotes leave out

A cheap quote is cheap because some parts are left out. Most of the time nobody tells you what those parts are, so you only find out later. Here is what I usually see missing.

The hosting is not in the price. It is **PKR 16,000–35,000 / year**, or it is locked on their account. The domain is on *their* email, not yours. There is no admin for you, so every small change needs a phone call. The “SEO” is only Yoast. There is no JazzCash / Easypaisa / COD. And on Shopify, you are not told about the dollar bill. On top of all this, the support is just “message me.”

A cheap package is only cheap if you ignore hosting and Shopify rent. Custom with lifetime hosting is the quote that stays honest.

So ask three things before you say yes. Is it WordPress, Shopify, or custom? Who owns the domain and hosting? What do I pay next year? If hosting is not lifetime, write **16,000–35,000** next to their number yourself. Then you can see what the quote is really worth.
