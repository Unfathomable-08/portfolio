## How a click actually dies here

Someone in Gulshan, DHA Lahore, or F-7 sees a kurta, a dental offer, or a biryani deal.

They tap.

Here is the usual path:

1. The ad opens WhatsApp, or a Facebook page, or Instagram.
2. They type “price?” or “size chart?” or “do you deliver to Johar Town?”
3. You are with a customer. You reply in 40 minutes.
4. They already messaged two other shops.
5. If they do buy, the order lives in chat. No pixel. Meta never learns who bought.
6. Next week you pay again to show the same person the same product.
7. If Facebook restricts the page, the shop is gone. You never owned the customers.

That is why Facebook ads are wasting money even with a “good” budget. The auction is not the whole story. The destination is.

Instagram ads fail the same way. Same company. Same pixel. Same dead end if there is no site.

### A night in the inbox (this is the job, not the ad)

You run the ad from 7pm because that is when Reels get cheap.

Between 9pm and 1am you get 40 messages.

Twelve are “this available?”

Eight send a screenshot of a different colour.

Six ask delivery to a city you do not cover.

Four want wholesale rates you only have in your head.

Two are ready to pay on JazzCash if you send the number.

You are closing the shop, or asleep, or on another chat. You answer at 11am. Half of them have already ordered from someone who sent a JazzCash screenshot at 9:12pm.

Ads Manager will still show “messages: 40.” It will look like a good night. It was not. The report cannot see the 11am graveyard.

A product page with price, size, city, and a pay option does not sleep. WhatsApp can still close the hesitant ones in the morning. The people who only needed a till already paid.

That is click to WhatsApp ads in Pakistan when they are the *only* system. The format is not wrong. The missing page is.

## Do you need a website for Facebook ads?

You can run ads without one. Click-to-WhatsApp works. Boosted posts work. A lot of first sales in Pakistan happen that way.

You cannot **scale** without one.

Without a site you cannot:

- install a Meta Pixel that fires Purchase or Lead on *your* page
- retarget people who viewed a product and left
- use the Sales objective properly (Meta needs events on a URL you control)
- show up when they Google your brand after the ad
- keep the shop if the page gets restricted
- take JazzCash / Easypaisa / bank transfer / COD on a checkout you own
- give a link that still works in a year

So: can you run Facebook ads without a website? Yes. Will they keep converting as you spend more? Usually no. That is the honest answer.

### What “testing without a site” should look like

If you have never spent a rupee on Meta, a week of click-to-WhatsApp on one product is a fair test. PKR 7,000–15,000. One city. One offer. See if anyone wants it.

That test answers “is there demand.”

It does not answer “can I run PKR 80,000 a month.”

The moment you decide the product sells, the next invoice should be the destination, not a bigger daily budget. Scaling WhatsApp-only is how shops get addicted to ads and still own nothing, no email list, no pixel, no Google presence, no backup if the page is restricted.

## The eight leaks (this is where the budget goes)

### 1. The ad sends them to WhatsApp only

Click-to-WhatsApp ads Pakistan-wide are popular for a reason. People here message. They do not love long forms. Cost per conversation is often in the PKR 20–50 range on simple campaigns, and a real enquiry can sit anywhere from a few hundred rupees to over a thousand depending on city and niche.

WhatsApp is the close. It is a bad *only* destination.

Problems I see every month:

- No record except the phone. Staff leave. The chat history is the business.
- You cannot retarget “people who saw the maroon lawn suit.” There is no product URL.
- Meta cannot tell a tyre-kicker from a buyer. Your Sales campaign is flying blind.
- Slow reply kills the lead. Ads run at 11pm. You sleep. They buy from the shop that answered.
- One number for ads, family, and old clients. Chaos.

Use WhatsApp **on the site**. Button on every product. Pre-filled message. You still talk in chat. You also have a page Meta can measure, and a URL people can share.

### 2. The ad sends them to a Facebook page

A page is not a shop.

It gets restricted. It gets hacked. Ads pause. Organic reach is not a store. The “Shop” tab is not JazzCash, not COD rules by city, not your wholesale price list.

Facebook page vs website in Pakistan is a false choice people make because the page is already there. The page is a notice board. The website is the shop. Ads should not point at the notice board.

If the page dies, everyone who only knew you on Facebook is gone. A domain you own still works. Google still has it. WhatsApp links still open it.

### 3. Instagram Shop is not a store here

Instagram Shop looks like checkout. In Pakistan it is not.

JazzCash and Easypaisa are not native. COD rules (Karachi vs upcountry, deposit on high AOV) are not native. Wholesale, size charts that match how we actually sell unstitched cloth, bank transfer screenshots, that is not Instagram checkout.

You can catalogue on Instagram. You should. Then the tap should go to **your** product page, not Meta’s cart.

Instagram ads that “don’t convert” are often converting into DMs. DMs are not a system. They are a queue.

### 4. No pixel, Meta is guessing

The Meta Pixel lives on a website. No site, no pixel. No pixel, no Purchase event. No Purchase event, the algorithm optimises for clicks and chats, not orders.

That is why you get likes and no sales. Engagement is cheap in Pakistan. Facebook has tens of millions of users here. People will react to a Reel. That does not mean they will pay.

WhatsApp chats also do not fire Purchase unless you stitch them with something extra (and most small shops never do). Ads Manager then says the campaign “failed” while your phone rang, or worse, Ads Manager says it succeeded on messages, and those messages never became orders.

If you want Facebook ads retargeting in Pakistan that actually cheapens over time, you need people to *hit a URL*. Product view. Add to cart. Thank-you page. That is how lookalikes get built from buyers, not from people who double-tapped a video.

### 5. You pay again for the same person

Someone saw the ad. They did not buy. In a proper setup they go into a 7-day or 30-day retargeting list. You show them a size chart, a review, a COD reminder. That audience is the cheapest you will ever buy.

Without a site, that list does not exist. Every impression is cold. You are always paying first-date prices.

### 6. They Google you after the ad

Pakistani buyers see the Instagram ad, then they search the brand name. Or they search “is this shop real.”

If Google shows nothing, or a dead Facebook page, or a WordPress site that looks like 2019, they do not come back to the DM.

Your ads are paying to send people into Google, and Google has nothing good to show.

### 7. The “website” is a theme

A lot of shops do buy a site, PKR 15,000–40,000 WordPress, or a Shopify trial. Then they run ads to it.

The theme is slow on mobile. Shopify bills in **dollars**, Basic plan alone about **PKR 95,000–130,000 a year** before apps. JazzCash is an add-on. WordPress hosting is **PKR 16,000–35,000 a year** if you want it to stay up.

Ads did not fail. The landing page failed.

Custom costs more on day one. You own the code. On [most sites I build](https://dev-muhammad.vercel.app/web-development), hosting is included for life. The URL you put in Ads Manager still works in year three.

### 8. Boost post is not a campaign, and the page can vanish

Boosting a photo from the app is not Ads Manager. You cannot retarget. You are paying for likes.

If Meta restricts the page, every boost and every ad stops. Do not keep your only shop inside an account you do not own.

## Likes but no sales

Likes are a cheap action. Meta will happily optimise for them if you pick Engagement or if you boost.

Sales need a clear offer, a place to act in one or two taps, a checkout that works at night, and a way to measure the sale.

If the creative is good and the likes are high and the orders are zero, the ad is entertaining people. Stop boosting. Point Messages or Sales at a page that can take an order.

Both. Site first. WhatsApp on the site.

You paid for engagement, or there is no till.
